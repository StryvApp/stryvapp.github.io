Stryv Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Stryv is a local-first health & fitness app. Your data lives on your device by default. This policy explains the small set of cases where data leaves your device, why, and how to delete it.

Data stored on your device

Workouts, sets, nutrition logs, water, walks and routes, journal entries, supplements, and goals are stored locally on your iPhone. If you never create an account, none of this is uploaded anywhere.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Stryv reads data such as steps, workouts, heart rate, sleep, body metrics, and blood glucose to power on-device dashboards and coaching. HealthKit data is processed on your device only. It is never uploaded to our servers, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

Optional account & cloud backup

You can optionally create an account (email and password, or Sign in with Apple) to back up your data and enable social features. When signed in, the following is stored on our servers (hosted by Supabase), protected by per-user access rules and encrypted in transit: workout logs, nutrition and water logs, walks and saved routes (which include location traces), journal entries, supplements, goals, and training progress. This data is used solely to back up and restore your app and to power the features described below — never for advertising.

Social features

If you connect with friends, Stryv shares only pre-aggregated daily summaries with the connections you approve — streaks, workout counts, and personal-record names — plus reactions and comments you post. Raw logs, health data, and location data are never shared with other users.

Food search

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, your search text or barcode is sent to food databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, and FatSecret — the FatSecret lookup goes through our server so API keys stay private). Search queries are used only to return results; they are not linked to your identity and we do not store them.

Location

With your permission, Stryv uses your location to build walking loops from where you are and to follow your position along a route, including while your screen is off during a walk. Route data stays on your device unless you have an account, in which case your saved routes and walk logs are included in your encrypted backup.

What we don't do

Stryv has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Deleting your data

Local data is removed by deleting the app. If you have an account, you can permanently delete it and all server-side data from within the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Children

Stryv is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
