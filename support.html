Stryv Support

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. An account is optional and only used for backup and social features. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or restore?

Create an account in Settings → Account. Backups happen automatically while signed in; restoring on a new phone replaces local data with your backup.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all server-side data immediately.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the three dots on any post, comment, or friend and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks the community rules is removed, and so is the account responsible. Blocking is immediate and the other person is not told. To undo a block, go to Friends, Manage friends, Blocked. You can also email a report to the address above.

Apple Health isn't showing data

Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stryv on your iPhone and make sure read access is granted.
